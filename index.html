About Me

I'm Harichandra Singh (Harish), an Agriculture Instructor, aspiring agricultural researcher, and digital content creator from Bajhang, Nepal. I am currently pursuing an M.Sc.Ag. in Agronomy while working as an Agriculture Instructor at Shree Veer Balbhadra Model Secondary School, Thabang, Rolpa.

I am the founder and creator behind Learn Krishi Official YouTube Channel, an educational platform dedicated to making agricultural knowledge accessible to students, farmers, agriculture professionals, and Lok Sewa Aayog aspirants. Through digital content, study notes, educational videos, and practical farming guides, I aim to connect scientific agricultural knowledge with real-world farming practices.

My academic background in B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture and practical exposure through agricultural internships, research-oriented activities, field visits, and agro-industrial training have strengthened my interest in crop production, agronomy, sustainable agriculture, agricultural extension, and modern farming technologies.

Beyond teaching, I am passionate about digital education, agricultural innovation, and developing technology-based tools that make learning and farming easier. Through my personal website and Learn Krishi YouTube Channel, I aspire to empower learners, support farmers, inspire young agricultural professionals, and contribute to the sustainable development of Nepal’s agricultural sector.

My mission is simple: to make agricultural knowledge accessible, practical, and useful for everyone.
